Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization (EIN: 42-0772566), and all contributions are tax-deductible. Donations play a crucial role in sustaining the cemetery, to complement unpredictable income from lot sales, burials, and earnings. Unrestricted donations help us meet the cemetery's urgent needs or secure matching grants for special projects. Restricted endowment gifts aim to provide ongoing income indefinitely.

We track each donor's cumulative contributions, whether from large or small donations. Our goal is to one day honor the many "Friends of Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery" who have generously supported us over the years.

Endowment Gifts
Donate to the Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery Endowment through the South Central Iowa Community Foundation (SCICF). The fund's principal remains intact, while a portion of its earnings supports ongoing costs and improvements. Any surplus income is reinvested to further grow the fund, ensuring your gift provides lasting support in perpetuity. You can mail a check or give online:
  • Mail check payable to SCICF with "Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery Endowment" in the memo to Rose Hill Cemetery, 531 W Main St, Lamoni, IA 50140, or mail it to SCICF, 705 Ilion Ave, Chariton, IA 50049.
  • Go to Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery Endowment to make an online donation. For larger donations, consider mailing a check rather than online for its full impact— online donations incur a 3% bank fee, reducing the amount we receive.
Please notify us of your donation so we can monitor its arrival and acknowledge it independently of SCICF. Email: LamoniRoseHill@gmail.com
Regular Contributions and Memorial Gifts
Mail check payable to "Rose Hill Cemetery" to Rose Hill Cemetery, 531 W Main St, Lamoni, IA 50140. Include a note with donor name(s) as they should appear, the acknowledgment address, and, if applicable, the name of the person being remembered. Before sending checks with specific project designations in the memo, please contact LamoniRoseHill@gmail.com for guidance. Note: Memorial Day Flags is a Lamoni Lions Club service project and is not funded by Rose Hill.
Donations from an IRA Account
If you're 70½ or older you can make a qualified charitable distribution (QCD) from your IRA to help satisfy your required minimum distribution (RMD) and reduce taxable income. Make checks payable to "Rose Hill Cemetery" for regular donations or "Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery Endowment" for endowment gifts, and mail to: Rose Hill Cemetery, 531 W Main St, Lamoni, IA 50140. Please notify us at LamoniRoseHill@gmail.com that a donation from your IRA is being sent.

Estate Gifts and Large Donations
Contact one of the officers for guidance on how to include Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery in your Will or Trust. We would entertain "naming" opportunities for cemetery roadways for extra large donations. To explore this contact one of the officers.
Volunteer Service
Volunteers are always welcome to pick up trash and debris. Broken branches can be moved out of the way or stacked under a tree. Gathered trash should be taken with you since we have no trash service. Volunteers with expertise and equipment can talk to us about larger projects (fill in & level sunken graves, seeding burial sites, box-scraping roads, chain saw work, etc). We even have need for volunteers doing on-line research to confirm that our records are complete and accurate. Contact LamoniRoseHillCemeterySexton@gmail.com.
