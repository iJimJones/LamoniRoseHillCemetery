Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery Plots For Sale

Boxed areas show where there are burial plots available for sale as of January 1, 2025

At Rose Hill the term space refers to a single grave space for burial; the term lot is an individually numbered and plated rectangle that consists of one or more spaces depending on its size; the term plot has no particular meaning and can refer to a single grave or a collection of them depending on context (e.g., the family plot).

Lots consist of multiple spaces and are sold by the space at $525 each. Only one full sized vault burial is allowed per space, but multiple cremains can be buried in a one space. West side SECTIONS have 4 foot wide spaces and east side BLOCKS have 3½ foot wide spaces.

Although a couple need only one space for their burials if they will be cremated, most will buy two adjacent spaces to accommodate a mid-size monument for the couple. The monument and its foundation must be at least 3 inches away from the plot's borders. Thus, on a 3½ foot wide burial space only a 3 foot wide monument and foundation is allowed. Typically, the monument itself is not as wide as the foundation/pad on which it sits.
