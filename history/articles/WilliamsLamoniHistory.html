History of Lamoni - by Thomas S. Williams (1958-65)

Thomas S. Williams, local history enthusiast, published some articles about Lamoni's early history in the Lamoni Chronicle. He served for years on Lamoni and county commissions and councils, and served as Mayor of Lamoni from 1942 through 1955. The links below display the page on which his article can be found. Use the page selector control at the top to view other pages of that issue. Not included here are the many "Bit of History" pieces written by him while mayor, mostly from what he found in the archives of city council minutes.
