This directory contains scans of deeds that describe property 
ownership in the lower half of Section 3, Township 67 N, 
Range 27 W, that are surrounding properties owned by the RLDS 
church and used for purposes unrelated to Rose Hill Cemetery. 
They are only here for historical interest since many of these 
transactions occurred at times when Rose Hill property 
transfers were also being made with the church or its donors.

There is a scan of the original deeds (some of them got cut 
off when copied) and a document with the text that is 
transcribed for easier viewing. Within the text documents, 
the formal descriptions are in blue.

Jim Jones
Lamoni Rose Hill Secretary/Treasurer
September 10, 2017
