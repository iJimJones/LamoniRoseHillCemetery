History of Lamoni - 1999 Newspaper Series by Alma Blair

Alma Blair, Professor of History at Graceland University, published a series in the Lamoni Chronicle entitled Countdown to 2000. Each of the 52 issues in 1999 carried an article he wrote on a different aspect of Lamoni's story. The links below take you to the opening page of the Chronicle that has the article. Many continue onto another page and let you know what page to go to. Be aware that photos or maps may exist on a later page without a note at the bottom to say that. To go to a different page of the same issue use the page controls in the panel above the viewed page.
