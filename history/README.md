# History Directory

This directory contains historical content about Lamoni Rose Hill Cemetery.

## Structure

- **articles/** - Historical articles about Lamoni and the cemetery
- **deeds/** - Historical deed documents
- **land/** - Land history and documentation
- **originStory/** - The origin story of Rose Hill Cemetery
- **pioneers/** - Information about cemetery pioneers and notable occupants
- **timeline/** - Historical timeline of the cemetery

## Content

The history section provides:

- Historical context and background
- Information about early settlers and pioneers
- Cemetery origin story and development
- Notable individuals buried at Rose Hill
- Historical documents and records

## File Formats

- HTML pages for narrative content
- PDF documents for historical records
- Images (JPG) for historical photos and documents
