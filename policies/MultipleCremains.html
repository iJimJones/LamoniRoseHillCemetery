Multiple Cremains per Space

Policy regarding multiple cremains burials in a single space

Approved: 5/8/2018

Multiple Cremains in One Plot

Up to four cremains burials are allowed per space (plot).

Note: Prior to this only two cremains burials were allowed in one burial plot or space.

Approved: 3/14/2017

Multiple Cremains in One Hole

Two cremains urns may be buried in a single grave opening, one over the other, at the price set for a large cremains opening.

Notes: The intent of this rule is to help the client save money in that the cemetery will only receive the standard fee for a single grave opening. The overall fee will be larger, that of a large cremains opening, so that the cremains digger will be compensated for the extra work to dig a larger or deeper opening as needed for the burial of the two small urns. It is the digger's prerogative to choose to dig a larger opening to place the urns side by side versus a deeper opening to place them one over the other.

In the case of extra-large non-standard urns, the digger's portion of the charge could be higher and involve backhoe digging. However, the cemetery will only add its standard fee for a single opening and pass that savings on to the client.
