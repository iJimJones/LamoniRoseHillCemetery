Decoration Rules

Rules and guidelines for decorations at Rose Hill Cemetery
Approved: 2/9/2016

All plantings, markers, or benches require prior approval by contacting the sexton1

Only permanent decorations2 are allowed during mowing season from March 15 to November 15, with the exception of special rules for Memorial Day and burials

Non-permanent decorations3 are allowed throughout the cemetery from May 15 to June 15, for Memorial Day

Non-permanent decorations3 are allowed for the first two weeks on a new burial site

Worn or improperly placed decorations may be removed by the caretakers

Keep dogs on a leash and dispose of dog waste responsibly outside of cemetery

The cemetery is not liable for loss or damage to vases or decorations

1 Sexton may be contacted by email at LamoniRoseHillSexton@gmail.com, by leaving a message at the Community of Christ Church office at 641-784-3353, or by visiting the church office at 531 West Main St., Lamoni, IA 50140

2 Permanent decorations are those attached to base stone or a permanent fixture, such as an attached vase, a wrought iron stand or shepherd's hook

3 Non-permanent decorations are those that are separate from the base stone, such as wreaths or containers placed on the ground

Important: Rose Hill Cemetery does not have trash service, so please return to pick up old decorations and other refuse to dispose of at home.

Become a volunteer or make a donation to keep Rose Hill Cemetery beautiful.
