Quick Start Guide - Lamoni Newspaper Archive

Quick Start Guide for Accessing the Lamoni Newspaper Archive Website:http://lamoni.advantage-preservation.com This guide is intended to be brief. For a more comprehensive guide, click on theResourcesmenu at the top of the webpage, or go directly to http://www.advantagearchives.com/resources/ For simplicity, you should either: - Searchfor something in the archive, or - Browseto a specific title on a specific date. Mixing the two can get confusing, so just click theHomemenu button at the top any time you need to return to this page and start afresh. HOW TO BROWSE TO A SPECIFIC DATE In this example we will selectThe Lamoni Chronicletitle, then navigate to the 8/18/1966issue that exists. Once you select any browse option, a left panel opens for you to select a decade, year, month, and day. - Left panellets you pick a day - Right panellets you pick which page to open Once a page is opened you can move to the next or previous page, zoom in or out, and use other controls. The highlighted days on the calendar are days in which issues exist based on your selections (if any). TheXbuttons in the left panel can be clicked to cancel and clear your chosen year or decade. You can also use the browser's back button to return to an earlier point in your selections. Note:Click title to open this page. Do not try to make sense of fragmented text. HOW TO SEARCH FOR SOMETHING In theSearch Forsection, enter the keywords and date range or use the default date range from first to last dates in the entire archive. In this example we want to find articles aboutWilliam Hopkinswho helped establish the Jefferson Highway in 1915 ± 5 years. We will drop his first name to find articles that excluded it. Illustrated below is a screenshot with the keywords and the calendars for selecting the date range. It may look odd selecting 1910 twice for start date below, but the first 1910 selected the decade of the 1910s and the second selected the year within that decade. Search Process: - Select Start Date: - Select decade - Select year - Select month - Select day - Select End Date:Similarly select a decade, year, month, and day - Click in the dates field to select a date range - Click either of the X buttons to cancel your date range - Click after selecting dates - Click after entering the words and date range The search result lists every page that has all the keywords on it, ordered from most to least hits of the keywords that are on the page. Note:Do not try to make sense of the fragmented text under each title. It is only useful for seeing the hits, i.e., keywords found on the page. In this example, there are12 hitson page 1 of The Lamoni Chronicle for November 18, 1915. Here is a part of the page shown when it is opened. Controls at the top let you go to different pages, zoom in/out, print clippings, and a magnifying glassFINDtool for the search and highlight settings. To make the highlighted keywords go away, use the magnifying glassFINDtool and delete the keywords from the search string.
