Veteran Flags & Crosses at Rose Hill
as of April 30, 2024
This lists all veterans with a flag or cross displayed on Memorial Day weekends, including many who are not buried here but grew up or had connections to Lamoni. This list is maintained by Gary & Shelly Waugh, who make and maintain the crosses. The Lamoni Lions Club puts the flags and crosses up each year for Memorial Day, and repair or replace damaged flags. Rose Hill publishes its own Veterans List of veterans who are buried or have a monument here.
