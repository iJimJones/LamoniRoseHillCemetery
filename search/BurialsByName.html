This is an alphabetical list of all who are deceased and were buried or have a monument here. A icon under FG is a link to their FindAGrave page. An "X" in the Vet column tags each veteran (see Veterans List for details). The Sect column gives the sectional ID, known as Sections on the west side and Blocks on the east– Block numbers begin with "B". The X & Y columns give lot coordinates within its section. X is the headstone row number W→E, and Y is the lot within that row N ↓ S. It's approximately 5 paces per lot, so Y=10 takes about 50 paces down that row to reach the lot. A Grid Map shows lots and their XY-coordinates. The burial list is available ordered by name and by location. Report errors to LamoniRoseHill@gmail.com.
Codes:
       C – cremains burial
       A – ashes scattered locally

       CP – cremains pending
       x2 – 2-in-1 burial

       NB – no burial (monument only)
       NS – no stone (unmarked grave)
       TM – Temporary marker (no stone)
